# Product Guidelines: PixelKeep

## Visual Identity & Aesthetic
PixelKeep embraces a multi-faceted 8-bit aesthetic that is flexible enough to evolve.
- **Cyber-Pixel:** High-contrast themes utilizing deep purples, blacks, and neon accents.
- **Fantasy RPG:** Warm, parchment-inspired tones and textures for a classic adventure feel.
- **Minimalist Pixel:** Clean lines and muted palettes for a professional retro experience.
- **Standard/Casual:** The architecture must remain decoupled from specific styles to allow for the future implementation of standard, non-pixel UI themes for a broader, casual audience.

## Tone of Voice
The application communicates with a blend of thematic immersion and professional efficiency.
- **Welcoming & Thematic:** Use RPG-inspired language (e.g., "Scribe Scroll", "Quest Log") to make the user feel like they are embarking on a journey.
- **Clear & Helpful:** Despite the thematic flair, functionality must always be clear. Instructions and feedback should be precise and easy to understand, acting as a reliable assistant to the user.

## Design Principles
- **Intentional Minimalism:** Every UI element must serve a purpose. Avoid clutter to maintain focus on the content.
- **Micro-Interactions:** Focus on tactile feedback, such as "pixel-perfect" hover states and smooth drag-and-drop animations, to enhance the "physical" feel of the app.
- **Accessibility:** While maintaining a pixelated look, ensure high contrast and readability. Support for modern, highly-readable fonts (like 'Exo') is mandatory for long-form scribing.
- **Privacy-First UI:** Never display sensitive content in plain text until the user has unlocked the vault. Ensure the encryption status is always visible but non-intrusive.
